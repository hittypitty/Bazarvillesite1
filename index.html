Frequently asked

Is there a minimum order quantity?+
Yes — MOQ is set per product/SKU and shown clearly on every product page, alongside the quantity at which bulk enquiry support kicks in.
How does bulk pricing work?+
Pricing is quantity-aware — as you increase quantity on a product page, pricing automatically recalculates to the applicable slab, and the total updates live.
Can I pay online?+
Not in this phase. Bazarville is a discovery and enquiry platform — commercial discussion, customization and payment happen over WhatsApp with our team.
What if a product is out of stock?+
Out-of-stock pages stay live with similar product suggestions, and you can still raise a WhatsApp enquiry for availability or replenishment.
