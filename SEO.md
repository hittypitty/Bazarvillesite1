# Bazarville SEO — what's done & what you do next

**Done in code:** sitemap (auto-includes all products at /sitemap.xml), robots.txt, canonical + OG/Twitter tags, unique titles/descriptions, one H1 per page, JSON-LD (Organization, WebSite, Product), alt text, clean URLs (no .html), https/HSTS + non-www → www redirect, OG image, noindex only on private pages (admin, pos, account, checkout…).

## Google Search Console (you do this once)
1. search.google.com/search-console → Add property → **URL prefix** → `https://www.bazarville.com`
2. Verify with the **HTML tag** method: copy the `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="...">` and paste it in the `<head>` of index.html (or send it to me and I'll add it).
3. Sitemaps → submit `sitemap.xml`. Then URL Inspection → Request indexing for `/`, `/products`, a few product pages.

## Backlink strategy (free, realistic)
- Google Business Profile for Bazarville (address, phone, products, photos) — biggest local win.
- Listings: IndiaMART, TradeIndia, JustDial, ExportersIndia, LinkedIn company page, Facebook, Instagram bio link (all with the www.bazarville.com link).
- Client case studies / "Client Stories": ask happy corporate clients to link back from their sites or LinkedIn posts.
- Guest posts / blogs: "Corporate gifting ideas for Diwali/New Year/onboarding kits" on HR & marketing blogs; tag Bazarville.
- Local/industry directories, HR & event-planner communities, supplier & printer partner pages (exchange links).
- Publish 1 helpful blog/guide per month on the site (gift ideas by profession/occasion) — gives others something to link to.
